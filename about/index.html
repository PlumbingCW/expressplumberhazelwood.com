About Hazelwood Express Plumbing.

Hazelwood Express Plumbing is a plumbing service based at 945 Hornet Dr in Hazelwood, Missouri. We take emergency and scheduled plumbing calls for Hazelwood and nearby St. Louis County towns.

Call (855) 575-2890

How the 24/7 line works.

You call (855) 575-2890 at any hour and reach the line, which stays open every day of the year. Tell us what is happening and where you are. We tell you what to shut off or stop using, then schedule the visit, with same-day appointments when available.

Before work starts, you get a clear price. If the job turns out to need something different, we explain why and agree on the new price before continuing.

Licensed and insured.

Plumbing work in St. Louis County requires a county plumbing license, and we use licensed plumbers. Permits for plumbing work in Hazelwood go through St. Louis County by way of the city, and we handle that paperwork when a job needs a permit.

You can and should check any plumber's license with the county before you hire. That goes for us as well.

What we do and do not do.

We handle drains, sewer lines, water heaters, leaks, fixtures, sump pumps and related plumbing for homes and small commercial properties. Our service pages describe each job in detail.

We do not repair foundations, rebuild roofs or replace electrical service. If a plumbing problem turns out to need a structural specialist or an electrician, we tell you so instead of guessing.

  • Gas leaks: leave the building and call Spire at 800-887-4173 or 911 first, then call us for the repair.
  • Public water mains and public sewers: Missouri American Water and MSD handle those.
  • Structural and foundation work: a structural specialist.

What a call looks like.

  1. You call. The line answers at any hour, and we ask for your address and what is happening.
  2. We guide you. If water is running or drains are backing up, we tell you what to shut off or stop using.
  3. We schedule. Same-day appointments are offered when available, and we confirm the timing on the phone.
  4. You get a price. After the plumber sees the problem, you hear the clear price before work starts.

Why the site is built the way it is.

You will not find a form on this site, and that is deliberate. A plumbing problem is easier to describe out loud than in a box, and a phone call gets you a real answer about what to shut off right now.

Every page has the phone number within easy reach, and the call button follows you down the page on a phone. If you would rather read first, the service guides and blog answer the common questions in detail.

The work we handle.

Drain and sewer work is the largest group, from drain cleaning and hydro jetting to sewer camera inspection and sewer line repair. Water heater work covers repairs, replacements and tankless units.

We also take leak detection, burst pipe repair, toilets, faucets, garbage disposals and sump pumps. Small commercial properties can call about commercial plumbing.

Every one of those pages says what we do and what we leave to other trades, so you know before you call.

Questions about whether we can help with your problem? Call and ask.

Who we work for.

Most calls come from homeowners in single-family houses, which is the bulk of the housing around Hazelwood. Hazelwood had about 11,800 housing units at the 2020 census, and older and newer homes alike run into the same handful of problems: slow drains, tired water heaters and leaks.

Landlords, property managers and small businesses call too. If you manage a rental, tell us on the call so we can plan access with your tenant, and you still get a clear price before work starts.

Our office is local, at 945 Hornet Dr in Hazelwood. The person who answers is there to get a plumber scheduled and to tell you what to do in the meantime, and you do not have to work through a long phone menu to reach them.

How we write about plumbing.

The service pages on this site explain each job in plain terms, including what the work involves and what usually changes the price. Where we quote costs, they are national ranges from public cost guides, labeled as national, because we do not publish rates we cannot stand behind.

Local facts come from public sources such as the City of Hazelwood, St. Louis County, Missouri American Water and MSD. When a fact is not available, such as the exact hardness at your tap, we say so and tell you how to find out.
