Plumber in Riverwood Trails, Hazelwood, MO.

Riverwood Trails is a residential community in Hazelwood that appears on the City's own street map. If a drain is backing up, a water heater is leaking or a pipe has failed in your home here, call and tell us your street. We will ask a few questions and schedule the visit.

What we can confirm about Riverwood Trails.

The City of Hazelwood's map of public buildings and streets, made by the City's GIS department in 2018, labels both Riverwood Trails and Riverwood Estates. A recent home listing on Riverwood Trails Drive places the house in the Riverwood Trails 2 subdivision, in Hazelwood Township and the Hazelwood School District. That listing gives 1978 as the year built.

It is one house, so we do not claim a build year for the whole community. Other listings show a condo unit on Riverwood Trails Drive, so the area mixes houses and attached homes. The house listing also mentions a yearly association fee for common areas, so ask your own association what it maintains.

That last point shapes the first phone call. If the trouble is inside your walls, it is yours. If it is a shared or outdoor line, the association or the sewer district may own it, and a quick check saves you paying for the wrong repair.

If your mail says Florissant.

Listings put some Riverwood addresses, such as Riverwood Estates Boulevard and Riverwood Park Drive, under Florissant with a 63031 ZIP, while Riverwood Trails Drive listings say Hazelwood. A mailing city is not always the city that issues your permits. Your tax bill or City Hall at 314-839-3700 can tell you which city you live in.

This matters because the city decides who handles permits and inspections for your job. Give us your street when you call and we will confirm we can schedule you. Our Florissant page covers the other side of that line.

How a visit to Riverwood Trails works.

A visit follows the same four steps, whatever the problem is.

  1. You describe the problem. Tell us which fixtures are affected, what you hear and whether water is on the floor.
  2. We confirm your address. Your street and cross street settle the city and the right visit.
  3. We give a clear price before work starts. The price comes after we see the problem, not before.
  4. We explain what we find. You hear what failed, what can wait and what cannot.

Local note

Utilities and permits for Riverwood Trails homes.

The City of Hazelwood names the Metropolitan St. Louis Sewer District for sewer and Missouri American Water for water. The private sewer line from the house to the public main is generally the owner's responsibility, so confirm yours with MSD at 314-768-6260 before paying for a repair you may not owe.

Missouri American Water's 2023 report put hardness at its Missouri River plants between roughly 5 and 13 grains per gallon. It does not say which plant serves which street, so test your own tap before buying a softener. The City says plumbing permits and inspections go through St. Louis County, and Inspections and Permits at 314-513-5034 can explain the process.
