What to do while you wait for a plumber.

The first ten minutes matter more than most people think. Find the main water shutoff, usually where the line enters the house near the water heater or in the basement, and learn which way it turns before you ever need it.

  1. Stop the water. Close the main valve for a burst pipe, or the fixture valve under a sink or toilet for a smaller leak.
  2. Protect the electrics. Keep away from wet outlets and appliances, and shut off power at the panel only if you can reach it dry.
  3. Stop using drains. If water is backing up, do not run the washer, dishwasher or showers, since each load adds to the backup.
  4. Smell gas? Leave the building and call Spire at 800-887-4173 or 911 before you call us.

After that, call the line and tell us what you see. We schedule the visit and tell you what else to do while you wait.
