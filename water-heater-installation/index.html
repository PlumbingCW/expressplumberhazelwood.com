What a complete installation includes.

A new water heater is more than a tank on a floor. The hookup has to meet manufacturer instructions and the plumbing code, and the parts around the tank matter as much as the tank.

  • New water connectors, shutoff valve and a drain valve.
  • A temperature and pressure relief valve with a discharge pipe run to a safe spot.
  • A drain pan where a leak could damage finished space.
  • Gas line, flue or electrical connection sized for the unit.
  • An expansion tank where the system needs one.

Skipping any of these is how small leaks turn into big ones. We list each part in the price before work starts.

Expansion tanks and why they matter.

When a heater warms water, the water expands. A system with a check valve or a pressure reducing valve has no place for that extra volume, so pressure rises and stresses the tank, the relief valve and the fixtures. A small expansion tank on the cold supply absorbs it.

Many homes need one and some do not. We test your static pressure and check for a backflow device before deciding. National guides list an expansion tank at $100 to $400 installed.

Local note

Code, permits and placement in Hazelwood.

The City of Hazelwood contracts with St. Louis County for plumbing permits and inspections, and plumbing work requires a St. Louis County license. That means your installation should be permitted through the county, with a licensed plumber on the job. A missing permit can come up when you sell, so ask for the paperwork.

Hazelwood publishes a frost depth of 30 inches and a winter design temperature of 2 degrees F. If the heater and its pipes sit in a garage or an outside wall, plan for insulation or a warmer location. Gas units in a garage also have rules about ignition height, which we check against the manufacturer instructions.

The City of Hazelwood lists the 2021 International Fuel Gas Code among the codes it has adopted, and plumbing permits and inspections run through St. Louis County. Under the International Plumbing Code (IPC) standard the county applies, plus the manufacturer's instructions, the fuel gas code and the county's plumbing rules, a permitted install has three sets of requirements to meet.

What happens on install day.

We set the tank, connect water, fuel and venting, and fill the tank before power or gas goes on. That order prevents burned elements and dry firing. Then we test the relief valve, check for leaks and set the thermostat.

If the old unit is still running, we haul it away. If you are comparing a tank to an on-demand unit, read about tankless water heaters. If something is already leaking, see water heater repair. We serve Maryland Heights and the other nearby towns too.
