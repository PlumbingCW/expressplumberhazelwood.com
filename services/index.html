Drains and Sewers.

Drain Cleaning.

Snaking and clearing for clogged kitchen, bath, laundry and floor drains, with a camera look when the clog keeps coming back.

Hydro Jetting.

High-pressure water jetting that scours grease, scale and roots from main lines when a cable only punches a hole through the clog.

Sewer Line Repair.

Repair of broken, collapsed or root-damaged sewer laterals by spot dig, pipe bursting or lining, plus cast iron pipe repair.

Sewer Inspection.

A planned sewer camera inspection with recorded video, used before buying a home or to find why a line keeps clogging.

Sewer Line Cleaning.

Cleaning the full sewer lateral from the cleanout to the public main, including root removal and a camera check of the result.

Water Heaters.

Water Heater Repair.

Diagnosis and repair for gas, electric and hybrid tank heaters that run cold, leak, rumble or trip a relief valve.

Water Heater Replacement.

Removal of a failed tank and installation of a new gas, electric or hybrid unit, with permit and code details handled.

Water Heater Installation.

Planned installation of tank water heaters, including venting, expansion tanks, gas or electric connections and St. Louis County permits.

Tankless Water Heater.

Installation, repair and annual flushing of gas and electric tankless water heaters, with hard water and venting guidance.

Leaks and Water Lines.

Leak Detection.

Finding hidden leaks behind walls, under slabs and in ceilings, plus help when the water meter spins and the bill climbs.

Repiping.

Replacing worn supply piping with PEX or copper, whole house or one section, when repairs stop holding.

Burst Pipe Repair.

Stopping the flood, repairing or replacing the split section, and thawing frozen lines the safe way.

Water Line Repair and Replacement.

Repairing or replacing the underground water service line between the meter and your house, including failed and leaking sections.

Commercial.

Commercial Plumbing.

Plumbing for offices, shops, restaurants and warehouses, including after-hours calls, backflow devices and grease line problems.

Fixtures.

Toilet Repair and Installation.

Repairs for running, clogged and leaking toilets, plus new toilet installs with a fresh seal and supply line.

Faucet and Sink Repair.

Repairs for dripping faucets, leaking sink traps and shutoff valves, plus faucet replacement in kitchens, baths and outdoors.

Garbage Disposal Repair and Installation.

Repair for jammed, humming and leaking disposals, and replacement or first-time installs under kitchen sinks.

Installation.

Plumbing Installation.

New showers, fixtures and supply or drain lines for remodels, additions and basements, with permits handled through St. Louis County.

Water Management.

Sump Pump Installation and Repair.

Sump pump repair, replacement and new installation, including float switches, battery backups and discharge lines that carry water away from the house.

Basement Waterproofing.

Interior waterproofing with drain tile and a sump pit to collect water at the footing and move it out of a damp or wet basement.

Water Softener and Filtration.

Water softener and filter installation and repair, with advice on hardness, chloramine and which system actually fits your water.

Planned work and urgent work.

Urgent problems get a different first step than planned ones. For a burst pipe, a sewer backup or a dead water heater, you call the 24/7 line and we schedule the visit, with same-day appointments when available.

Planned work such as a new water heater, a repipe or a sump pump upgrade starts with a conversation about options, then a clear price before work starts. Permits for plumbing in Hazelwood run through St. Louis County, and we handle that paperwork when a job needs one.
