How to check who owns the problem.

Before a plumber digs, it helps to know whose pipe is failing. Water that is wrong at the meter is usually the utility's problem, while a leak on the house side belongs to the property owner.

Sewers follow a similar rule. The public main is maintained by MSD, and the private lateral from the house to that main is generally the owner's responsibility. Confirm your own case with MSD at 314-768-6260 or Missouri American Water at 866-430-0820.

If you are unsure, call us first. We can tell you from the symptoms which side of the property line is likely at fault, and which utility to contact. That one conversation can save you from paying a plumber for a problem the utility should fix.

Seasonal problems that cross town lines.

Cold weather hits the whole area at once. The City of Hazelwood publishes a 30 inch frost depth and a winter design temperature of 2 degrees F, so water lines and unheated spaces such as garages and crawl spaces face the same freeze risk in every town we serve.

January 2024 showed what that looks like. Missouri American Water crews answered 16 water main breaks in a single day, and plumbers reported that pipes burst as things thawed. Know where your main shutoff is before the next hard freeze, not during it.

Water quality is the other shared factor. Missouri American Water reported hardness at its Missouri River plants of roughly 5 to 13 grains per gallon in 2023, which is moderately hard to very hard. Scale builds up in water heaters and fixtures over time, so test your own tap or ask the utility for its water quality report.

Pipes frozen or a water heater failing in the cold? Call the 24/7 line.
