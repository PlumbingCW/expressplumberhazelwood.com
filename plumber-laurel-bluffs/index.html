What plumbing from the mid-2000s needs next.

Plumbing that is 20 years old is not failing by default. It is old enough that the parts with a limited life have probably used it up. Tank water heaters often last 8 to 12 years, so an original unit is well past that range, and one that was replaced may be nearing it.

  • A water heater older than ten years, or one with rust at the base or a damp floor around it.
  • Toilets that run on or refill slowly, which often means worn rubber parts.
  • Shutoff valves under sinks that will not turn or weep when moved.
  • Washing machine hoses that are hard, bulging or more than a few years old.

Rubber parts matter here because Missouri American Water treats its water with chloramines. That does not harm copper, but flappers and seals still wear. A worn toilet part is a toilet repair, and an aging heater is a decision for water heater replacement before it fails.

A walk-out basement and the lowest drain in the house.

At least one Laurel Bluffs listing mentions a walk-out basement. If your lower level has a bathroom, laundry or floor drain, that is the lowest opening in the house. In a main line backup, sewage rises there first, so a gurgling floor drain is a warning worth a call.

Keep floor drains clear and do not store anything you cannot lose near them. If one backs up, stop using water in the house. A camera look through sewer camera inspection shows whether roots or a belly in the line are the cause.

Common ground behind your lot.

A lot that backs to common ground means trees and drainage outside your property line may belong to the association. Roots from large trees can reach a sewer lateral, but the lateral that serves your house is generally yours. Ask your association what it maintains, and who to tell if you see standing water behind your lot.
