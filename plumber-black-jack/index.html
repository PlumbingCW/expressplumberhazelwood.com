What a small city's housing numbers tell you.

Black Jack had 6,634 residents in 2020, down from 6,929 in 2010, and 2,747 housing units, 8.8 percent of them vacant. With a small, long-established housing base, many Black Jack homes may still have original pipes. If your house has slow drains in more than one fixture, that is usually the main line, and we check it with a camera before suggesting work.

The city was named for a grove of blackjack oaks. Mature trees are common in older suburbs, and roots are the usual suspect when a main drain clogs again and again. The camera tells us whether roots have entered a joint, which decides between cleaning the line and repairing it.

Water heater and appliance gas connections follow the manufacturer's instructions and the fuel gas code the local authority has adopted, commonly based on the NFPA 54 standard or the International Fuel Gas Code. Ask the county which edition applies to your address before gas work begins.

Common Black Jack calls and where to read more.

Most calls fall into a few patterns. A repeat clog is a job for sewer inspection, a cold shower often means water heater repair, and a bill that climbs for no reason calls for leak detection. Several slow drains at once usually point to drain cleaning on the main line.

Cold snaps and your pipes.

In January 2024, Missouri American Water crews responded to 16 water main breaks in a single day during a cold snap, and one local plumber reported more than 180 calls in a day. The advice from that week still stands. Know where your shutoff is, and expect trouble when pipes thaw. Frozen lines usually split as they thaw.

If a frozen pipe is still frozen, open the faucet, close the main and call. For a pipe that already split, see burst pipe repair.
